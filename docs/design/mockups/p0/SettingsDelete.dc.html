Three different actions, three different words

The Accounts menus offer Archive… first and Delete permanently… last; Settings holds deletion and erasure. Each has its own title, button text and typed phrase.

Reversible

Archive an account

  • Leaves the account lists and becomes read-only.
  • Trades, reviews, rulebooks and history are kept.
  • Restore from archive brings it back as it was.
From: Accounts row menu, account detail ⋯ menu. Simple confirmation.
Permanent

Delete one account

  • Removes one attempt and everything recorded for it.
  • Linked attempts and shared data stay.
  • Files you exported are never touched.
From: Settings, Accounts menus. Preview, then type DELETE ACCOUNT <id>.
Permanent · everything

Erase the entire journal

  • Every account, trade, review, rulebook and attachment.
  • Also FundedFlow's own recovery copies.
  • FundedFlow starts empty, like a new install.
From: Settings only. Type ERASE ENTIRE JOURNAL.
