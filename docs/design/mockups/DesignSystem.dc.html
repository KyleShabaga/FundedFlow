Patterns these screens add (not in the design system yet)

Sidebar

Collapsible 248 → 72 px rail with groups, counts and a local-save status. Shared component on this canvas.

DataTable

Toolbar, sortable headers with aria-sort, row selection with bulk actions, mini meters, page size and pager.

MonthYearPicker

Popover with a year stepper and 12 P&L-tinted months; future months disabled.

DayTimer

Countdown to the account's trading-day reset; blue, amber at 1 h, red at 15 min, grey when closed.

StatList

Label and value rows for Reports and the selected-day panel.

Stepper

Numbered steps with done and current states, used by Import.

RuleState

Rulebook states beside RuleMeter: Needs setup, None, Off, Manual check, rulebook verified or needing review.

Popover

Menu surface on shadow-pop for the picker, timer resets and column chooser.
