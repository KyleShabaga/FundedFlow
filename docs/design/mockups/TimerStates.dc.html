Day-ending timer

Counts down to the account's trading-day reset (its time zone and reset time), the moment daily loss limits start over. It lives in the top bar on every screen.

Normal · more than 1 hour

Brand blue on a soft ground. Quiet enough to ignore while you trade.

Last hour

Amber from 60 minutes: time to decide whether to take another setup.

Last 15 minutes

Red from 15 minutes. Firms that flatten positions at the reset close them here.

Weekend

Grey while closed. Weekend activity counts toward Monday's trading day.

Behaviour

  • One account selected: counts to that account's reset, in its own time zone (for example 5:00 PM America/Chicago).
  • Several accounts: shows the soonest reset; clicking it lists each account (right).
  • At zero the label changes to "New trading day" for one minute, and the dashboard's daily-loss meters drop back to zero.
  • Screen readers hear the time on focus only; it never announces every second.
  • Hover shows the exact reset: "Your account's trading day resets at 5:00 PM CT. Daily loss limits reset then."
