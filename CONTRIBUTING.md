# Contributing to FundedFlow

FundedFlow's original software and associated documentation are licensed under the [MIT License](LICENSE).
Unless you explicitly state otherwise, a contribution intentionally submitted for inclusion in FundedFlow
is submitted under that same license. Submit only work you have the right to contribute; identify third-party
material and retain its applicable license and notices. A separate copyright transfer or contributor agreement
is not required by this project.

Use a focused pull request that explains the change and how it was verified. For planned work, link the owning
roadmap card and follow the scope and checks in [CLAUDE.md](CLAUDE.md). See [README.md](README.md) for setup.
Changes to dependencies must preserve their licenses and regenerate the shipped third-party notices.

Do not put real journals, trading CSVs, account identifiers, screenshots containing personal information,
credentials, or other private data in issues, pull requests, or test fixtures. Use synthetic examples.

The MIT license for the code does not grant rights to third-party pages or documents processed by the collector.
Its existing permission and fetching safeguards continue to apply.
